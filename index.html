Detection Layers
1 · Momentum Burst
Price moves ≥0.15% in 3 ticks — catches the initial thrust of every spike event.
2 · Volatility Expansion
Candle range ≥ 3.0× ATR(14) — abnormal volatility = spike fingerprint.
3 · Wick Rejection
Wick ≥ 65% of candle range, small body — classic spike-and-reject exhaustion.
4 · Statistical Outlier
Z-score ≥ 2.5σ from SMA(20) — pure mathematical edge.
5 · RSI Divergence
New price extreme + weakening RSI — spike losing fuel, highest-confidence reversal signal.
Prediction Layers
P1 · ATR Compression (30%)
Current ATR shrinks below 40% of its rolling average — market coiling before an explosive move.
P2 · Overdue Pressure (20%)
Tick count since last spike vs session-calibrated average. Above 80% = high pressure. Above 100% = critical.
P3 · RSI Positioning (20%)
BOOM: RSI drifting down toward oversold. CRASH: RSI drifting up toward overbought. Directional filter.
P4 · Pattern Memory (15%)
Session-calibrated interval from last 5 real spike gaps. More accurate than the fixed average.
P5 · Tick Velocity (10%)
Abnormal change in tick arrival rate. Slowdown before BOOM spikes, speed-up before CRASH spikes.
P6 · BB Squeeze (5%)
Bollinger Band width narrows below 30% of its rolling average — classic pre-breakout compression signal.
Best setup: P1 (ATR compress) + P6 (BB squeeze) both active + P2 overdue >80% = highest confidence pre-spike setup.
BOOM → wait for spike, enter RISE after. CRASH → wait for spike, enter FALL after.
